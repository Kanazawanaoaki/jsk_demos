Wash off any dirt. (Potatoes with uneven surfaces tend to retain dirt, so wash them thoroughly.) (Avoid using potatoes that have turned green.)
Remove the sprouts. Cut them into uniform sizes. (Once you're comfortable handling a knife, you can peel them before boiling.)
Add enough water to cover the potatoes, cover the pot, and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce the heat. (High heat → Low heat)
Transfer to a colander once a bamboo skewer pierces them easily. (Why use a bamboo skewer?)
Peel them after they've cooled. (Be careful not to burn yourself.)
