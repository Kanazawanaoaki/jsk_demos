Rinse the leaf creases and soil from the base using stored water or running water.
Once the water boils vigorously, add the greens starting from the base. (Boil a generous amount of water) (High heat)
Plunge the greens into cold water. (Once the water boils again, turn off the heat and submerge them.)
Squeeze out excess water. (Gently squeeze while keeping the stems aligned.)
Cut with a knife. (Cut into 4-5 cm lengths.)
