Measure the rice and wash it. Wash the measured rice by stirring it while changing the water 3-4 times. Rice absorbs water even while being washed, so wash it quickly. Transfer the washed rice to a colander and drain the water.
Measure the water and let the rice absorb it. Place the drained rice and measured water in a pot and let it absorb well. (For at least 30 minutes. This time is a guideline for cooking 4 servings in a standard pot.)
Cook. Light the burner and bring to a boil over high heat. When the lid rattles and steam rises, reduce to medium heat. When the water has evaporated and the lid stops moving, reduce to low heat.
Steam. Turn off the heat and steam. Do not open the lid for about 10 minutes. Gently fluff the rice, cover with a dry cloth, and replace the lid.
Serve, taste, and clean up. Serve and taste. Soaking dishes and the pot in water makes them easier to wash.
