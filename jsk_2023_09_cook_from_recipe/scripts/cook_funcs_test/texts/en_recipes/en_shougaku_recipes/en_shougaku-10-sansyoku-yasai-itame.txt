① Wash. Lightly scrub the carrots and bell peppers. Transfer the washed vegetables to a colander and drain.
② Cut. Slice the carrots into 4cm rounds, then cut vertically into 5mm thick pieces. Julienne into 5mm strips. Halve the bell peppers and remove seeds. Julienne into 5mm strips. Remove the core from the cabbage and cut into 4cm pieces. Cut into 1cm strips.
③ Stir-fry. Heat oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Once hot, swirl to coat the pan. Add ingredients in order (carrot, bell pepper, cabbage) and stir-fry quickly. Season with salt and pepper, then remove from heat.
④ Plate. Plate immediately after cooking.
