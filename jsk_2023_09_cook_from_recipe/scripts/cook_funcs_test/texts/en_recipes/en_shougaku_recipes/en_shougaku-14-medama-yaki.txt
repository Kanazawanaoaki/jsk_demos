① Crack the eggs into a bowl.
② Add oil to a frying pan and heat over medium heat.
③ Once the pan is hot, swirl the oil to coat the surface, then gently slide the eggs into the pan.
④ When the whites change color, add 1 tablespoon of water, cover, and reduce heat to low.
⑤ Plate the boiled potatoes and broccoli, then season to taste.
