1. Tear the lettuce into large bite-sized pieces. Slice the tomato into 5mm thick rounds.
2. Arrange the bacon in a frying pan over medium heat. Cook, flipping occasionally, until golden brown on both sides. Remove from the pan.
3. Toast the bread. Spread mayonnaise and ketchup on one side of each of the two slices. Layer bacon, lettuce, and tomato on one slice, then top with the other slice.
4. Wrap in parchment paper, place a weight on top, and let sit for about 15 minutes. Cut while still wrapped in the parchment paper.
