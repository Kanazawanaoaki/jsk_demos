1. Peel the onion and place it on a cutting board. Cut it in half lengthwise with a knife, then trim off the top and bottom. Place it cut-side down, cut it in half again, then cut each half in half. Repeat with the remaining onion.
2. Peel the carrot with a peeler, place it on the cutting board, and cut off the stem end with a knife. Hold the thicker end and slice into 2cm-thick rounds starting from the thinner end.
3. Wash the potato, peel it with a peeler, and remove any sprouts. Place it on the cutting board, hold it steady with your hand to prevent slipping, and cut it in half with a knife. Place it cut-side down and cut each half in half again.
4. Add salad oil to a pot and spread it evenly. Add the chicken and the vegetables from steps ①, ②, and ③. Place the pot on the stove over medium heat. Hold the pot handle with one hand and stir-fry for about 1 minute.
5. Add the measured amount of water. Once it boils and bubbles start to form, skim off the fine, light brown foam with a ladle and discard it into a bowl of water. Cover and simmer over low heat for 10-15 minutes.
6. Turn off the heat, add the curry roux, submerge it in the broth, and let it sit for about 2 minutes. Once softened, stir to dissolve.
7. Return to medium heat. Hold the pot handle with one hand and stir with a ladle for about 3 minutes until thickened. Pour over rice in bowls.
