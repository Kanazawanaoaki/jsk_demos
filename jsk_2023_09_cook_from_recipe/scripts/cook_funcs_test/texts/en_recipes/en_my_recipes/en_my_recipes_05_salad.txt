1. Slice the carrots into 5mm rounds and cut the broccoli into bite-sized pieces. Boil them, then drain in a colander.
2. Boil the cabbage, remove the core, and cut into 1-2cm wide strips. Thinly slice the core for use.
3. Arrange the boiled carrots, broccoli, and cabbage on a plate and top with your preferred sauce.
