1. Slice the carrots into thin strips. Cut the bell peppers in half lengthwise, remove the stem and seeds, then cut each half crosswise into 4 pieces. Remove the core from the cabbage and cut into 3cm squares.
2. Heat oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Once hot, swirl to coat the pan. Add ingredients in this order: carrots, bell peppers, cabbage. Stir-fry quickly.
Season with salt and pepper. Remove from heat.
3. Serve immediately after cooking.
