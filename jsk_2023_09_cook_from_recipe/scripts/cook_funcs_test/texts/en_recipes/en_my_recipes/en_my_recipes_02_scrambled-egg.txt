1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them lightly.
2. Add the milk and salt, then mix.
3. Heat salad oil in a frying pan over medium heat for about 2 minutes, then add butter.
4. Once the butter has melted, quickly pour in the egg mixture and wait about 20 seconds.
5. When the edges and bottom start to set, quickly stir with a wooden spatula 30 to 40 times.
6. Once the mixture is evenly creamy, turn off the heat and quickly transfer to a serving dish.
