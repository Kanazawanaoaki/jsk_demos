Add enough water to cover the eggs, then cover the pot.
Place the pot on the stove and bring to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce to low heat and simmer for 10-12 minutes. (If the eggs are dirty, wash them before use.)
Remove the lid, take out the eggs, cool them in water, then peel them. (Peeling them in water makes it easier.)
Slice the eggs. (You can also use a wire or egg slicer.)
