1 Slice the eggplant into rounds and soak in water. Cut the onion into wedges, the bell pepper into bite-sized pieces, and the bacon into thin strips.
2 Heat oil and garlic paste in a pot. Add the bacon and vegetables and sauté.
3 Once the vegetables have softened, add the whole tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper, cover, and simmer over low heat for 10 minutes.
