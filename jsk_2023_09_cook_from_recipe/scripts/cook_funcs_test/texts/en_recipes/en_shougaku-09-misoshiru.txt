① Prepare the broth. Fill a pot with water and add the dried sardines with heads and guts removed.
② Prepare the ingredients. Wash the vegetables. Cut the ingredients. Slice the daikon radish into 5mm-thick half-moon pieces. Thinly slice the green onions. Cut the fried tofu into thin strips.
③ Make the broth. Bring the pot with the dried sardines from step ① to a boil over high heat. Once boiling, reduce to medium heat and simmer for 4-5 minutes. Remove the sardines after the broth is extracted. You can also leave them in and eat them as part of the dish.
④ Simmer the ingredients. Add the harder ingredients or those that take longer to cook first. Add the daikon radish. Once the daikon is translucent, add the aburaage and green onions.
⑤ Add the miso and turn off the heat. Add the miso. Once it comes back to a boil, turn off the heat immediately. Do not add all the miso at once; taste as you go. Overheating will make the flavor harsh and cause the aroma to dissipate.
